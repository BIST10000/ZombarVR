Leather-Strapped Potion Bottle 3D model

A heavy round flask of blue liquid held in a harness of stitched leather straps, finished with a cork stopper. The glass, liquid and leather all have their own material, which makes it a showpiece item rather than background clutter.

What you can do with it

  • A health or mana potion pickup in an RPG
  • An inventory or shop icon rendered on a plain background
  • A close-up hero prop on an alchemist's table

Performance: is 58,040 triangles a lot?

Medium. Best as a hero or close-up prop. Make a decimated copy for distant views or mobile. It has fewer triangles than 18% of the 52 models in this library. Read more about polygon budgets, or how to reduce a model's polygon count.

Before you use it

At over 58,000 triangles it's heavy for a small item. Decimate it for mobile, or use it only where it's seen up close.
